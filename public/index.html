Frequently asked questions

Is the Friends Talk protocol completely free?

Yes. Voice streaming, text teletype, global filters, and peer bookmarking are 100% free with zero paywalls.

Do I need an account or cryptographic wallet?

No. No login, no email, and no wallet required. You receive an ephemeral session handle that dissolves when you leave.

Are audio calls recorded on servers?

No. Audio streams directly between user devices via WebRTC peer-to-peer connections and never touches server storage.

How are malicious users handled?

Tap Block to instantly drop the connection and permanently prevent that peer from matching with your device again.

What age restrictions apply?

The protocol is strictly restricted to adult humans aged 18 and older. Minors are prohibited from utilizing this network.
