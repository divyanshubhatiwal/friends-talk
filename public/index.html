Protocol Architecture

Engineered for resilience and total privacy

Built without central audio storage, biometric profiling, or camera surveillance.

Non-Custodial Audio

Audio packets travel directly between participant browsers over end-to-end encrypted WebRTC channels. The server never hears a byte.

Automated Defense Grid

Text and image transfers pass through automated neural safety filters. One-click permanent blocking persists across sessions.

Zero Signup Overhead

No email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords. Your identity is an ephemeral session token that dissolves on disconnect.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Friends Talk protocol completely free?

Yes. Voice streaming, text teletype, global filters, and peer bookmarking are 100% free with zero paywalls.

Do I need an account or sign up?

No. No login, no email, and no phone number required. You receive an ephemeral session token that dissolves when you leave.

Are audio calls recorded on servers?

No. Audio streams directly between user devices via WebRTC peer-to-peer connections and never touches server storage.

How are malicious users handled?

Tap Block to instantly drop the connection and permanently prevent that peer from matching with your device again.

What age restrictions apply?

The protocol is strictly restricted to adult humans aged 18 and older. Minors are prohibited from utilizing this network.
